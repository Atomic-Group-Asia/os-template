# Lessons

The corrections file. Loaded every session, alongside the master instructions.

Two kinds of entry:

1. **Rules** — preferences. When you say "always do X" or "never do Y", it
   becomes a numbered rule here, permanently.
2. **Lessons** — things learned the hard way. What went wrong, why, and what
   to do instead.

## How it works
- A correction gets added here **immediately**, not at the end of the session
- One line per entry, dated
- Every rule carries its *why* — a rule without a reason is a rule that gets
  dropped the first time it's inconvenient
- This file is the canonical place for corrections. Nowhere else
