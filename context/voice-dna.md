# Voice

How things sound when they go out under our name.

Anything written for a customer, a partner, or the public gets checked against
this file first.

## Tone
Where we sit between formal and casual, warm and direct, playful and serious.
Pick a point, not a range — "professional but friendly" describes almost every
business ever and steers nothing.

## Words we use
The vocabulary that sounds like us. Product names spelled the right way, terms
we prefer, phrases that recur.

## Words we never use
The banned list. Jargon, clichés, claims we can't legally make, anything that
makes us sound like everyone else. This section usually does more work than
the one above it.

## Anti-voice
What we must never sound like. Name it specifically — a competitor, a genre of
post, a type of salesperson. "Don't sound like X" is easier to follow than any
positive instruction.

## Real samples
Paste two or three things actually written by us that sound right — an email,
a caption, a message. Label each one. Samples beat adjectives: the AI can
imitate a paragraph, it can only guess at "authentic."
