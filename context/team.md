# Team

One block per person. This file answers a question the AI gets asked
constantly: **who do I go to for this, and who decides?**

Keep it to people whose work the AI needs to route around or write for. It is
not an HR record — no salaries, no personal data, no performance notes about
individuals.

---

## [Name]

- **role** — their title, and the plain-English version of it
- **owns** — the areas, brands, or channels that are theirs. Be specific enough
  that "who owns this?" has one answer
- **decides** — what they can call on their own, without escalating
- **reports to** — who
- **works with** — the handful of people they're in the loop with daily
- **reachable on** — the channel they actually answer on

### How to work with them
Output preferences, language, level of detail, what frustrates them. If the
AI drafts anything that goes to this person, this is what shapes it.
