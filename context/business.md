# The Business

The entity. What it is, how it makes money, where it stands.

Brands live in `brands.md`, people live in `team.md`, routes to market live in
`channels.md`. This file is the level above all three — if something is true of
only one brand, it belongs in that brand's block, not here.

## What it is
One sentence a stranger would understand, then a paragraph for anyone who
needs more.

## How money works
Who pays, for what, how often, and what it costs us to deliver. Name the
model plainly — product sales through retail, subscription, project fees,
retainer, marketplace commission.

## Stage and size
Roughly where the business is: years running, headcount, revenue band if
you're willing to write it down. The AI calibrates its advice on this — the
right answer for a five-person business is the wrong answer for fifty.

## The market
Who else is in this space, what they do differently, and where we genuinely
win. Skip the flattery — a competitor section that says everyone else is worse
is a section nobody will read twice.

## What's hard right now
The real constraint. Cash, capacity, a channel that stopped ordering, a
product that won't stick. Naming it here means the AI stops proposing things
that ignore it.
