# Brands

One block per brand. Copy the block, fill it, repeat. If there is only one
brand, keep one block — the shape still holds when a second one appears.

A brand earns a block when it has its own buyer, its own price ladder, or its
own promise. A product line that shares all three with an existing brand is a
section inside that brand, not a new one.

---

## [Brand name]

**One line** — what it is, in a sentence a stranger would understand.

**Status** — live / launching / paused / retired.

### Who it's for
The buyer for *this* brand, not the company's buyer in general. Age, life
stage, what's going on in their life when they reach for this. If two brands
here have identical buyers, one of them is probably a product line.

### The promise
What it claims to do for them, in their words. The thing that makes someone
choose it over the shelf next to it.

### What we sell
| SKU / offer | Price | Margin | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
|  |  |  |  |

### Where it sells
Name the channels, and link to the matching block in `channels.md` rather than
repeating the terms here. A brand that sells everywhere and a brand that sells
in one chain need very different decisions.

### How it's promoted
The mechanics that actually work for this brand — the promo shapes, the
channels, the seasons. Not the campaign calendar; the pattern behind it.

### What we know that isn't obvious
The hard-won stuff. Why a past launch failed. Which claim can't be made
legally. Which retailer hates which pack size. This section is the reason
the file is worth keeping.

### Open questions
What we still don't know about this brand. Being honest here is more useful
than filling the section above with guesses.
