# About Me

The person running this. Written in first person, so the AI can read it and
know whose shoes it's standing in.

## Identity
Name, role, where you're based, what you're responsible for. One paragraph.

## What I actually do all day
Not the job title — the real work. The meetings, the decisions, the things
that eat your week. Be specific: "I approve every piece of packaging artwork"
tells the AI more than "I oversee marketing."

## What I'm good at
The things people come to you for. Where your judgement is worth trusting.

## What I'd rather not do
The work you want handed off. This is the list the AI should be looking for
chances to take off you.

## How technical I am
Plainly: have you written code, used a terminal, set up software before? The
AI adjusts how much it explains based on this. Under-claiming here costs you
nothing; over-claiming costs you an hour of confusion.

## What I'm trying to build
Where this is going in the next year. The AI uses this to tell the difference
between work that matters and work that's just loud.
