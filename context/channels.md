# Channels

Every way a product reaches a buyer. One block per channel.

The shape is always the same: **someone or something stands between you and
the buyer, and takes a cut for it.** A retail chain, a marketplace, a
distributor, a reseller agent, an AI agent that sells on your behalf, or
nobody at all (direct) — all of them are instances of that one shape.

When a new kind of channel appears, add a new `type:` value. Do not add a new
file, and do not add a new heading to this one.
